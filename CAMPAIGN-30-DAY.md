# Protect What You Built — 30-Day Campaign

## Campaign objective

Generate qualified 15-minute Protection Reviews for POP Financial Group across Maryland, Washington, DC, and Virginia. The campaign promotes education and an individual review—not a particular product, return, rate, tax result, approval, or guaranteed outcome.

Primary CTA: **Start My Free 15-Minute Protection Review**

Campaign pages:

- Families: `https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/family-protection.html`
- Retirement: `https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/retirement-income.html`
- Business owners: `https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/business-protection.html`

## 30-day operating target

| Metric | 30-day target |
|---|---:|
| New direct conversations | 300 |
| Partner introductions requested | 40 |
| Qualified reviews booked | 60 |
| Reviews held | 42+ |
| Applications submitted | 14–20 |
| Placed-and-paid cases | 11–16 |

These are operating targets, not promised results. Track placed-and-paid revenue and acquisition cost by source. A $40,000 month will likely require a mix of life, higher-premium business, and suitable annuity cases rather than ordinary life policies alone.

## Weekly execution rhythm

### Monday — Family protection

- Publish one 30–45 second educational video.
- Contact 15 parents, clients, or warm prospects.
- Ask five current relationships for one introduction.
- Send traffic to the family landing page.

### Wednesday — Retirement readiness

- Publish one 30–45 second educational video.
- Contact 15 professionals, public employees, or people aged 45+.
- Follow up with every retirement lead from the previous 14 days.
- Send traffic to the retirement landing page.

### Friday — Business continuity

- Publish one 30–45 second educational video.
- Contact 10 business owners and five referral partners.
- Visit or call one local business group, barbershop, real-estate office, tax professional, or mortgage professional.
- Send traffic to the business landing page.

### Every business day

- Respond to new leads within five minutes when possible.
- Complete 10 follow-ups.
- Update source, appointment, application, placement, and revenue status.
- Post one Story with a question, poll, or behind-the-scenes clip.

## Social content: 12 ready-to-publish posts

### Post 1 — Campaign launch

**On-screen hook:** If your income stopped tomorrow, how long would your plan last?

**Spoken script:** Most people do not avoid protection because they do not care. They avoid it because nobody has helped them make the numbers simple. POP Financial Group is offering a free 15-minute Protection Review to help families understand what they have, where a gap may exist, and which options fit their budget. No pressure—just clarity.

**Caption:** Protecting your family starts with understanding the gap. Start your free 15-minute review: https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/family-protection.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=launch

### Post 2 — Mortgage question

**On-screen hook:** Life insurance is not only about funeral costs.

**Spoken script:** If something happened to you, would your family also have to worry about the mortgage, childcare, debt, and replacing your income? A protection review looks at the entire responsibility—not just one bill.

**Caption:** Coverage should be connected to real responsibilities. Review yours here: https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/family-protection.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=mortgage

### Post 3 — Existing coverage

**On-screen hook:** Having a policy does not always mean having the right amount.

**Spoken script:** Your income, mortgage, children, debt, and goals can change. That is why existing coverage should be reviewed—not automatically replaced, but understood. Know what it does, how long it lasts, and whether it still fits.

**Caption:** Already insured? A review can still be valuable. No replacement is recommended without a clear reason and comparison.

### Post 4 — Personal story

**On-screen hook:** I learned that financial protection becomes personal very quickly.

**Spoken script:** My father-in-law passed away one month before my wedding. Our family felt the emotional loss and the financial responsibility at the same time. That experience is one reason I believe protection conversations should happen before a crisis—not during one.

**Caption:** Purpose before pressure. Education before sales. Start here: https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/family-protection.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=story

### Post 5 — Retirement income gap

**On-screen hook:** Retirement is not only about how much you saved.

**Spoken script:** The bigger question is how your savings, pension, Social Security, and other resources may work together to support your expenses. A retirement-income review helps organize those moving pieces before you make a product decision.

**Caption:** Turn savings into an income conversation: https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/retirement-income.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=income_gap

### Post 6 — Annuity education

**On-screen hook:** An annuity is not automatically good or bad.

**Spoken script:** The right question is whether a particular contract fits your timeline, liquidity needs, goals, and tolerance for risk. You should understand surrender periods, fees, income features, limitations, and the insurer behind the guarantees before deciding.

**Caption:** Learn the trade-offs before making a decision. Guarantees depend on the issuing insurer's claims-paying ability.

### Post 7 — Five-year question

**On-screen hook:** Planning to retire in the next five years?

**Spoken script:** This is the time to identify expected expenses, dependable income sources, market exposure, and money you may need to access. Waiting until retirement day can reduce your choices.

**Caption:** Start a pressure-free retirement review: https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/retirement-income.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=five_years

### Post 8 — Beneficiary check

**On-screen hook:** When did you last review your beneficiaries?

**Spoken script:** Marriage, divorce, children, business changes, and deaths in the family can make old beneficiary decisions outdated. A review does not need to be complicated, but it should not be ignored.

**Caption:** Add a beneficiary review to your annual financial checklist.

### Post 9 — Key person

**On-screen hook:** Which person would be hardest for your business to lose?

**Spoken script:** A key person may drive revenue, operations, financing, or client relationships. If that person did not return, would the business have enough time and capital to recover? That is a continuity question worth answering.

**Caption:** Business owners: start your review here: https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/business-protection.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=key_person

### Post 10 — Buy-sell funding

**On-screen hook:** A buy-sell agreement without funding may only be a plan on paper.

**Spoken script:** The legal agreement explains what should happen. The funding strategy helps determine whether it can happen. Your attorney and tax professional handle their areas; an insurance review can help evaluate appropriate funding options.

**Caption:** Coordinate the legal document with a practical funding conversation.

### Post 11 — Owner perspective

**On-screen hook:** I do not talk to business owners only as an insurance agent.

**Spoken script:** I have owned a barbershop for more than 15 years. I understand payroll, rent, team responsibilities, and how much a business can depend on one or two people. That is why my first question is not, “What product do you want?” It is, “What are you trying to protect?”

**Caption:** Business-owner to business-owner: https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/business-protection.html?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=owner_story

### Post 12 — Direct invitation

**On-screen hook:** Three questions. Fifteen minutes. More clarity.

**Spoken script:** Who depends on your income? What financial obligation concerns you most? When did you last review your current plan? If you cannot answer all three confidently, schedule a free Protection Review with POP Financial Group.

**Caption:** Choose your path at https://www.popfinancialgroup.com/?utm_source=social&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=protect_what_you_built&utm_content=direct_invitation
